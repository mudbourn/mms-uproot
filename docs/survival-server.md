# mms-uproot: group "survival-server"

Method: jars unzipped (never run) into `work/<slug>/`. A small Python class-file reader dumped the mixin targets, `method=` and `target=` strings, and every `net/minecraft` class a mod references. These were mapped to Mojang names with the layered 1.21.11 mappings in the Gradle cache. Then checked against the NeoForged 26.1/26.2/26.3 porting primers (github.com/neoforged/.github/primers), the Fabric 26.1/26.2/26.3 blog posts and Fabric's `fabric-api-26-1-migration-map.xml`. Modrinth and GitHub were used for ports and source. No 26.x jar was downloaded or inspected. Primer greps are heuristic, so "no hit" is not proof of "unchanged".

## Findings that apply across the group

1. **No refmaps.** Every jar here has the intermediary names written directly into its annotation strings (`@Mixin(class_1887)`, `method="method_8192"`, `target="Lnet/minecraft/class_332;method_52706(...)"`), `@Shadow`, `@Accessor` and `@Invoker` names, and `class_NNN` descriptors. Immersive Optimization declares a refmap but doesn't ship one. So uproot must rewrite the strings inside the mixin classes themselves, not a refmap. This only works if the string-to-mapping step covers descriptors inside `target=`, `@At`, `@Slice` and `@Coerce`. `mixins.json` `compatibilityLevel` is JAVA_21 (MAIT, IO), but 26.x is Java 25, and all the 26.x ports set JAVA_25.
2. **Access wideners are intermediary-namespace** (`accessWidener v1 intermediary`) in thermoo, frostiful and scorchful. Thermoo's 26.1 changelog talks about classtweakers, so the format and namespace may need converting. Entries: `Gui$HeartType`, `Projectile.<init>` (`extendable`), `Biome$ClimateSettings`.
3. **Plain remap is not enough: many 26.x changes are member and class renames the intermediary-to-official mapping can't know.** Hits in this group (primer):
   - 26.2: `EntityType.X` static fields move to `EntityTypes.X`, and the same for `BlockEntityType` to `BlockEntityTypes`. Almost every mod here reads `EntityType.TURTLE`, `STRAY` and the like.
   - 26.2: `Slime` and `MagmaCube` move to `monster.cubemob`. `EntityType.create(Level, EntitySpawnReason)` becomes `EntitySpawnRequest`.
   - 26.1: `ChunkPos.toLong` becomes `pack`. `Entity.getTags` becomes `entityTags`. `GuiGraphics` becomes `GuiGraphicsExtractor`, and the GUI `render*` methods become `extract*` (Thermoo's 26.1 source targets `Gui.extractHearts`, `extractHeart` and `extractVehicleHealth`). `WaterlilyBlock` becomes `LilyPadBlock`. `BlockRenderDispatcher` is removed.
   - 26.3: `Entity.hurtMarked` becomes `syncVelocity`. `Entity.setInvulnerable` becomes `setPermanentlyInvulnerable`. `EnderMan` becomes `Enderman`. `LivingEntity` swing fields are merged into `swingState` and made private. `EntityRenderer.shouldRender` gains a partial-tick parameter. `LivingEntity.getVisibilityPercent` takes a `ServerLevel`. `LevelRenderer.doEntityOutline` becomes `blitEntityOutline`. `GameRenderer.render()` loses its arguments. `Block.codec` and the block_type registry are removed.
   - 26.3: `AxeItem`, `HoeItem` and `ShovelItem` are removed. Their behavior moves to `DataComponents.BLOCK_TRANSFORMER`.
4. **Fabric API renames and removals.** Fabric publishes a 139-entry class map for 26.1. Used here: `ParticleFactoryRegistry` becomes `ParticleProviderRegistry`; `BlockRenderLayerMap` becomes `ChunkSectionLayerMap`; `EntityModelLayerRegistry` becomes `ModelLayerRegistry`; `LivingEntityFeatureRendererRegistrationCallback` becomes `LivingEntityRenderLayerRegistrationCallback`; `ComponentTooltipAppenderRegistry` becomes `ItemComponentTooltipProviderRegistry`; `FabricItemGroup` becomes `FabricCreativeModeTab`; `LandPathNodeTypesRegistry` becomes `LandPathTypeRegistry`; `ClientCommandManager` becomes `ClientCommands`. Removed in 26.3: `FabricBrewingRecipeRegistryBuilder`/`FabricPotionBrewingBuilder` (brewing recipes are now datapack JSON), `FuelRegistry`, `CompostingChanceRegistry`, and the Strippable/Tillable/Flattenable registries. Frostiful, Scorchful and MAIT use the brewing builder, so uproot needs a shim that turns those registrations into generated brewing recipe JSON. Frostiful's `GameRuleBuilder` and `CustomGameRuleCategory`, and Leashable's `GameRules.registerBoolean`/`registerInteger` invoker plus `<clinit>` inject, all depend on the game rule system. It was reworked in 26.1 (`GameRuleMap` saved data), so these need checking.
5. **Mods that subclass or construct vanilla classes.** Fake AFK has `FakeNetworkHandler extends ServerGamePacketListenerImpl` and a `FakeConnection`. Its 26.1 port deleted that subclass and changed `PlayerList.loadPlayerData` to return an `Optional<CompoundTag>` read through `TagValueInput`. Leashable has `LeashProxyEntity extends Turtle` and overrides `tick`, `getHealth`, `registerGoals` and `push`. A remap layer can't translate these. Per-mod patching, or a newer build, is the only route.
6. **Jar-in-jar libraries need bridging too.** Peek's nested Patbox libs target 1.21.9 to 1.21.11 (`placeholder-api 2.8.1+1.21.10`, `server-translations-api 2.5.2+1.21.9-pre3`). Upstream Peek v2.1.0 note: `player-data-api` has no 26.3 build, but its two mixin targets are unchanged in 26.3.
7. **Dependency availability on 26.3 (Modrinth):** present for Carpet, Cardinal Components, Cloth Config, YACL, Xaero's World Map and Minimap, Fabric Language Kotlin, Placeholder API, fabric-permissions-api, LuckPerms, Mod Menu. Missing: owo-lib (last build 26.2; all seven Expanded Enchanting mods need it), Colorful Hearts (26.2, optional for Frostiful).
8. **Data and resource side.** Only Frostiful and Scorchful carry worldgen data. 26.3 moves `worldgen/configured_feature` to `worldgen/feature`, with the config object flattened into the feature. Frostiful has 3 configured and 3 placed features, 24 template pools, structure NBT, trial spawners and 22 worldgen tags. Scorchful has 3 configured and 2 placed features, a structure set, a structure, processor lists and 27 worldgen tags. Those JSON files need a converter. Loot tables and conditions (number providers split into float and int providers), structure NBT (upgraded through the DataVersion), tags, advancements and recipes are probably accepted unchanged, but I did not verify this. Frostiful 438 data files, 544 assets; Scorchful 166 and 172; Thermoo 6 and 7; MAIT 67 and 14. Client assets have the 26.x render-pipeline changes to cover (the other group findings handle the client side). Expanded Enchanting, Fake AFK, Leashable, IO, Peek and MapSyncer have no datapack content.

## Per mod notes

**thermoo.** Mixins hit `Gui` (hearts HUD, `@Local(name="xo"/"yo")` locals), `LivingEntity.createLivingAttributes`, `ItemStack.forEachModifier`, `Entity.tick`, `DataComponentMap$Builder`, `ItemAttributeModifiers$Builder`, and the datafix class `AttributeIdPrefixFix`. Also uses Fabric `DynamicRegistries`/`FabricRegistryBuilder`, `ArgumentTypeRegistry`, `ResourceCondition`, and optional Polymer. Thermoo 10.x is a breaking API release (packages versioned, Yumi `TriState`, registry classes renamed), and Frostiful and Scorchful depend on it, so the three move as a set. The 26.1 branch already holds the remaps. On top of those, the Gui HUD retargets (a 26.2 split of `Gui` into `Gui` and `Hud`) and the 26.3 renames remain. The jar's three-mixin client side is the only real risk. Needs: stay with 10.1.6 for 26.1.2 and bridge 26.1 to 26.3, or bridge 9.1.0 with the Gui retargets plus CCA 26.3.

**frostiful.** Biggest surface in the group. Client mixins hit `LivingEntityRenderer.submit` and `extractRenderState` (uses `BlockRenderDispatcher`, removed in 26.1), `GameRenderer.bobView`/`renderItemInHand`, `CapeLayer.submit`, `WingsLayer`, `HumanoidMobRenderer`, `ArmorStandRenderer`, `PolarBearRenderer`, and render-state classes. Server mixins hit `Entity`/`LivingEntity` movement (`maybeBackOffFromEdge`, `aiStep`, `causeFallDamage`), `ServerLevel.tickChunk`, `ServerChunkCache.tickChunks`, `BlockBehaviour.entityInside`, `PatrolSpawner`, `Raid.spawnGroup` (uses `EntityType.create`, whose signature changed in 26.2), and datafix lambdas (`GameRuleRegistryFix.method_76071` has no Mojang name). Uses optional Trinkets, Mod Menu and Colorful Hearts. 3.1.0 gameplay and config changes (new content since 2.4.0 likely) are unchecked. Needs: use the `26.1.x` build as base plus the 26.2 and 26.3 deltas; if bridging 2.4.0 instead, expect hand-written render shims.

**scorchful.** Client mixins hit `GameRenderer.render`, `Gui.renderCameraOverlays`/`renderHeart`, `EntityRenderer.shouldRender` (26.3 signature change), `CustomHeadLayer.submit`, `HumanoidArmorLayer`, and `HumanoidMobRenderer.extractHumanoidRenderState`. Server mixins hit `LivingEntity.getVisibilityPercent` (26.3 signature change), `Consumable.onConsume`, `AbstractThrownPotion`, `ServerLevel.tickChunk`, `TargetingConditions`, and `PoiTypes.TYPE_BY_STATE`. 0.18.3 embeds Immersive Storms and adds Dust and Red Dust content, and its entry points are now Yumi. Needs: same set-wide approach as thermoo.

**expanded-*-enchanting (7).** One mixin per jar: `@ModifyReturnValue` on `Enchantment.canEnchant` and/or `Enchantment.areCompatible`, reading `Enchantments.*` keys and `ItemStack.getItem()`. The upstream 26.1 port made no code changes in the mixin (only build files). The 26.2 commit only renamed a parameter. Neither primer lists any change to `Enchantment` or the weapon item classes. The single known 26.3 break is Axe: `instanceof AxeItem` has to become `itemStack.is(ItemTags.AXES)` (or a `BLOCK_TRANSFORMER` check). Needs: the generic remap, an `instanceof AxeItem` rewrite shim, owo-lib and FLK present on 26.3 (owo-lib isn't yet).

**fake-afk.** See pattern 5. The 1.21.11 jar already has known-broken inventory recovery (author note on the 1.21.9 build). Carpet 26.3 exists. Needs: take the 26.1 build (claims 26.2) and bridge to 26.3, or patch out the `FakeNetworkHandler` path.

**leashable-players.** Mixins hit `Entity.canUsePortal(boolean)`, `BlockAttachedEntity.skipAttackInteraction`, `LeashFenceKnotEntity.interact`, `Player.interactOn`, `ServerPlayer.tick`/`die`/`disconnect`, `Turtle.readAdditionalSaveData`, and `GameRules` `<clinit>`. Residual 26.3 work on top of 1.3.0+26.2: `hurtMarked` to `syncVelocity`, `setInvulnerable` rename, `EntityType.TURTLE` to `EntityTypes.TURTLE`. 1.3.0 is a multiloader restructure, and its README corrects the gamerule names, so check the gamerule ids haven't changed for existing worlds.

**peekmod.** No mixins. A pure API consumer: `CommandSourceStack`, `ServerPlayer`, `PlayerList`, `TeleportTransition`, `BossEvent`, `Component`/`Style`, plus the bundled libs. Upstream rewrote it for 26.1+ (Java 25, Fabric API required) and tagged 26.3 on 2026-10-07. Needs: nothing but dropping in the `peek-2.1.0+26.3.jar` GitHub release asset (not on Modrinth, which stops at 26.2).

**mob-ai-tweaks.** Touches nearly every mob class, 15 `@ModifyArg`, 15 `@ModifyConstant`, 11 `@ModifyExpressionValue`, 10 `@ModifyReturnValue`, 1 `@Redirect` (`VillagerEntityMixin`), plus its own AI-goal subclasses (`SpearUseGoal`, `ChargeSpearGoal`, ender dragon phases and others). Direct 26.3 hits: the `EnderMan` to `Enderman` rename, `Slime`/`MagmaCube` package move, `LivingEntity` swing fields now private, `Entity.setInvulnerable` rename, `ArmedEntityRenderState.attackTime` to `swingAnimation`, and `OrderedSubmitNodeCollector.submitModel` changes. Upstream's only 26.x build is `1.11.0-beta` (26.2), whose own notes say a lot is broken, and the repo has no 26.x source. Needs: per-mixin retargets across ~100 targets plus 33 client render mixins. Skip it or wait for an upstream 26.3 release.

**immersive-optimization.** The 26.2 branch differs from 1.21.11 only in the following. `TickScheduler`: `ChunkPos.toLong()` becomes `pack()`. `client.LevelRendererMixin`: retargeted from `prepareCullFrustum` to `render`, now reading `CameraRenderState.cullFrustum` and `Matrix4fc`. The mixins json: refmap dropped, JAVA_25. The six server mixins (`EntityTickList`, `ServerLevel.tick`/`tickNonPassenger`, `TicketStorage.getAllChunksWithTicketThat`, `Level.shouldTickBlocksAt`, `MinecraftServer.loadLevel`) were unchanged through 26.2, and no primer mentions them in 26.3. Needs: the 26.2 jar, plus disabling or retargeting the client mixin (26.3 changed `LevelRenderer.render` again); on a dedicated server it never loads.

**mapsyncer-for-xaeroworldmap.** No mixins. It reads region (`.mca`) files by path and already contains a "26.1+ new format" `dimensions/<ns>/<name>/region` detector alongside the legacy `DIM-1`/`DIM1` one. Block-family `instanceof` and tag logic (~90 vanilla classes) is where renames bite (`WaterlilyBlock` to `LilyPadBlock`; `Block.codec` removal is harmless here). It needs Xaero's World Map (26.3 present) and Fabric API. Upstream publishes fabric jars for 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3 (1.0.4beta2 is a GitHub release, beta). The license is unclear (see the table).

## Not verified

- No 26.x jar was opened, so the 26.2 to 26.3 deltas are inferred from the primers and blog posts only. In particular, whether the `Gui` heart methods (`extractHearts`, `extractHeart`, `extractVehicleHealth`) moved into `Hud` in 26.2/26.3, and whether `Gui$HeartType` and `Biome$ClimateSettings` still exist.
- Thermoo, Frostiful and Scorchful: I did not diff their 1.21.11 and 26.1.x branches in full (GitHub's compare capped at 300 files). What the 3.1/0.18 releases change in gameplay or config is unknown.
- Whether `@Local(name=...)` captures in Thermoo/Frostiful survive (they depend on local-variable names in the 26.x vanilla jar).
- Game rule system after 26.1: whether Leashable's `GameRules.registerBoolean`/`registerInteger` invoker and Frostiful's `GameRuleBuilder` still work as written.
- Whether 26.3 accepts the old loot, structure-set and number-provider JSON without conversion.
- MapSyncer's real license; MAIT 26.2 beta's actual quality; owo-lib's plans for 26.3; Fake AFK's and Leashable's 26.2 jars on 26.3 (both are source-compatible by reading, but unrun).
