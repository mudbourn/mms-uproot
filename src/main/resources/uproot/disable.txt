# --- Biomes We've Gone: pumpkin warden (blocks.md R36) ---
# Not disabled any more: its brain is built on 1.21.11 Brain calls, which the stand-ins of shim/entity (LegacyBrainMob, LegacyBrains)
# answer, and the burrow's occupants are the same mob. Its renderer needed the context GeckoLib 5.5's item layer asks for, which a
# ctor rule of renames.txt passes.
# Known limits, with no rule to lift them:
# - The warden's day schedule is the timeline biomeswevegone:pumpkin_warden. No tag in the mod's data lists it, so no dimension runs it and the
#   mob stays on its core and idle activities (panic after a hit still works). Adding it to data/minecraft/tags/timeline/in_overworld.json
#   makes it follow the schedule and shelter in a burrow at rest time (tried on a server).
# - Map marker colour: 26.3's MapDecorationType has no colour and the map_color item component is gone (MapColorComponentRemovalFix
#   deletes it), so the explorer maps keep the vanilla tint. The constructor adapter in renames.txt drops the argument.

# --- CorgiLib (blocks.md section 3.6 and 7) ---
# /placeall runs: its two changed calls are answered by LegacyWorld (see renames.txt).
# The debug sphere and ring helpers (client/RenderUtils) are not disabled: every render call they make exists in 26.3 (the audit lists none
# missing). Nothing in CorgiLib calls them, and nothing has drawn with them on a client yet.
# The scroll hook of the structure box editor (MixinMouseHandler) runs: its target call is Gui.overlay() now (see renames.txt).

# Map decoration tint (R32) needs no rule: the remapper's constructor rule drops the colour argument.

# --- Biomes We've Gone: surface rules ---
# 26.3 replaced SurfaceRules with material rules; Uproot's shim/surface stands in for the old API and converts what a
# mod builds, so the rule sources and the TerraBlender call run (see renames.txt). What does not carry over is below.
# The mixin on the 1.21.11 surface system does two jobs. It gives the bands rule source the surface system's seeded random and
# secondary noise (the stand-in system of shim/surface has both, and the mixin is applied to it), and it injects the
# eroded-badlands pillars into the surface pass for two biomes. 26.3 has no such pass to inject into (renames.txt marks the
# injector optional), and Uproot's own MaterialSystemMixin restores the pillars.
