# --- Biomes We've Gone: surface rules ---
# 26.3 replaced SurfaceRules with material rules; Uproot's shim/surface stands in for the old API and converts what a
# mod builds, so the rule sources and the TerraBlender call run (see renames.txt). What does not carry over is below.
# The mixin on the 1.21.11 surface system gave two biomes the eroded-badlands pillars and let the bands source reach
# the system's noise. The stand-in system is not that class, so the mixin is left out; Uproot's own MaterialSystemMixin
# restores the pillars, and the bands source falls back to plain stone.
mixin net/potionstudios/biomeswevegone/mixin/SurfaceSystemMixin | patches 1.21.11's surface system; pillars come from Uproot's MaterialSystemMixin, bands fall back to stone

# --- Biomes We've Gone: extra village houses ---
# The mod pushes its houses into the vanilla village pools by writing the pools' private lists. 26.3 gives no way to add to a
# built pool, so the vanilla villages stay as they are; BWG's own village structures (village/*) are separate and unaffected.
noop net/potionstudios/biomeswevegone/world/level/levelgen/structure/village/PlaceInVillage addBuildingToPool * | villages cannot take extra houses: the pool lists are private in 26.3
